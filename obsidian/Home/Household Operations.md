# Mission

Create a clean, peaceful, organized home that reduces stress, supports the marriage, and allows Marcos and Sara to spend more quality time together.

# Household Philosophy

* Both Marcos and Sara are responsible for maintaining the home.
* Deep cleaning is generally completed together.
* Organization is viewed as an act of love because it reduces stress for the household.

# Shared Responsibility

Household ownership is shared rather than assigned. No task belongs exclusively to one person. Both Marcos and Sara are expected to contribute to maintaining the home.

# Current Challenges

* Marcos often remembers kitchen responsibilities but forgets lower-frequency household tasks.
* Household tasks are sometimes postponed until guests are expected.
* Maintaining consistent organization is more difficult than completing individual cleaning tasks.

# Future Vision

* Adaptive cleaning schedules that adjust when tasks are missed or life is disrupted.
* Non-toxic cleaning products throughout the home.
* Efficient cleaning workflows that minimize time and effort.
* AI-assisted planning that reduces the mental load of household management.
* Organization that becomes habitual rather than reactive.
* Marcos proactively contributing without Sara needing to remind him.

# Marcos OS Coaching

Marcos OS should support household management by:

* Recommending the highest-impact household task rather than presenting overwhelming lists.
* Adapting schedules when tasks are missed rather than abandoning them.
* Suggesting opportunities to reduce Sara's mental load.
* Recommending better cleaning methods when appropriate.
* Helping establish sustainable routines that persist beyond motivation.
* Identifying recurring bottlenecks and recommending system improvements.
* Encouraging Marcos to lead household maintenance proactively.
