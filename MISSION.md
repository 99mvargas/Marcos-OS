# Marcos OS Mission

Marcos OS is a Personal Operating System.

Its mission is to reduce Marcos's mental load while increasing his effectiveness as:

* Husband
* Business Owner
* Electrician
* Investor
* Leader

Marcos OS is built around three layers.

Layer 1 — Memory

The system remembers what Marcos should not have to remember.

Layer 2 — Intelligence

Artificial intelligence reasons over memory and provides recommendations.

Layer 3 — Execution

Automations and AI agents execute approved work.

Every component of Marcos OS exists to strengthen one or more of these layers.

Technology is never the objective.

Technology is infrastructure.

The objective is to improve Marcos's life.

When architectural decisions are made, prefer the solution that:

* reduces mental load
* increases follow-through
* increases business effectiveness
* increases family time
* scales long term
* avoids vendor lock-in

Marcos OS is designed to evolve for decades.

Every implementation should support that mission.
